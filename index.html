🍼 research / babylm.ipynb

          

            

              

                
BabyLM Challenge

                
Undergraduate Researcher · Data Science Capstone (DSC 180) · UC San Diego

              

              Sep 2026 → Mar 2027
            

            
How much can a language model learn from roughly the amount of language a child hears by age 13? I'm working on sample-efficient pretraining under BabyLM's developmentally plausible data budget.


            

              
BabyLM budget
100M words

              
≈ what a child hears by age 13. Big LLMs train on trillions of tokens.

            


            
my idea: let the model sharpen its own learning signal

            
A self-improving objective that combines self-distillation from an EMA teacher with dynamic loss-based sample reweighting, so no external pretrained teacher and no extra data are needed.
